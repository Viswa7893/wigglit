Privacy Policy

Short version: Wigglit doesn't collect your data. Everything stays on your Mac.

Last updated: 26 October 2026

What Wigglit collects

Nothing. Wigglit does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

What is stored on your Mac

To work, Wigglit saves the following only on your Mac, in the app's local preferences:

  • Your settings (reminder intervals, schedule, character, sounds and similar options)
  • The name you choose to enter, if any, so the buddy can greet you
  • Your reminder stats (how many times you answered Yes or No each day)

This information never leaves your Mac and is not accessible to us. You can delete it at any time from Settings (Clear stats, or Reset ALL settings), or by uninstalling the app.

Away detection

If “Skip reminders when I'm away” is on, Wigglit asks macOS how long it has been since the last keyboard or mouse input. It only reads this single number to decide whether to show a reminder. It does not record what you type or where you click, and it does not store or send this information.

Children

Wigglit does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example if a future version adds a feature that needs data, we will update this page and the date above before that version is released.
