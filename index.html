Getting started

I opened Wigglit but nothing appeared. Where is it?

Wigglit lives in the menu bar at the top-right of your screen, not in the Dock. Look for the small buddy icon and click it to open the panel.

On MacBooks with a notch, menu bar icons can hide behind the notch when the bar is crowded. Quit a few other menu bar apps, or hold ⌘ and drag icons to make room.

How do I see the buddy right now?

Click the menu bar icon, then under Remind me now click Drink water, Rest your eyes or Stretch. The buddy walks onto the screen where your mouse pointer is.

How do I open Settings?

Click the menu bar icon, then click Settings at the bottom of the panel.

Reminders

Reminders aren't showing up

Wigglit skips a reminder on purpose in these cases. Check each one:

  • Paused: the menu bar panel shows a Pause/Resume button. Make sure reminders are active.
  • Active hours: Settings → Schedule. If “Only remind me during these hours” is on, reminders only appear inside that time window and on the selected days.
  • Away detection: if you haven't touched the keyboard or mouse for a while (5 minutes by default), Wigglit assumes you're away and skips that reminder.
  • Turned off: Settings → Reminders. Each reminder can be switched off individually.
  • Interval: by default water is every 45 min, eyes every 20 min and stretching every 60 min. Changing a setting restarts that timer.
How do I change how often a reminder appears?

Settings → Reminders. Each reminder has its own interval and color.

What happens when I click Yes or No?

Yes counts toward your daily stats and the buddy celebrates. No makes the buddy sad, and if “Come back sooner if I say No” is on (Settings → Behavior) it asks again after a short delay. If you don't answer, it gives up and walks away.

Can I write my own messages?

Yes. Settings → Reminders. Enter one message per line for the question, the Yes reply and the No reply. Leave a box empty to use the built-in messages.

Customizing

Change the character, size or speed

Settings → Characters. Choose from five buddies, set walking speed and direction, size, height above the Dock, and which display it appears on.

Turn the sound off or change it

Settings → Behavior → Sound.

Start Wigglit automatically when I log in

Settings → Behavior → turn on Launch Wigglit at login. You can also manage it in macOS System Settings → General → Login Items.

Reset my stats or settings

Stats: Settings → Stats → Clear stats. Settings: Settings → Behavior → Reset ALL settings to defaults. Both ask for confirmation and can't be undone.

Removing Wigglit

How do I quit or uninstall?

To quit, click the menu bar icon, then Quit. To uninstall, quit the app and drag Wigglit from your Applications folder to the Trash. If you turned on launch at login, turn it off first in Settings → Behavior, or remove Wigglit from System Settings → General → Login Items afterwards.
